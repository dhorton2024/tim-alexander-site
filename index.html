About

Practical Skills, Honest Work

Alexander Mechanical is a hands-on mechanical and light engineering service based in the Marlborough region. The team will tackle anything mechanical — no job is too small, and they take pride in getting it done right the first time.

Whether you need a small engine back in action, a trailer repaired or built from scratch, or a good waterblast — the team can come to you or you can bring the work to the property.

Small Engines Specialist

Motorbikes, lawnmowers, and everything in between — small engines are a specialty.

Trailers Built & Repaired

New build trailers and trailer repairs. From a bent drawbar to a full custom build.

Light Engineering

Fabrication, welding, and general mechanical work. If it's broken, bent, or needs building — we can sort it.

Honest Workmanship

Straight-talking, no-bullshit work. You'll know exactly what's happening and why.
